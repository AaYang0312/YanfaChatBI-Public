The scenario uses two commercially prominent recent games rather than inventing a "most popular among students" ranking: [Capcom's March 16, 2026 announcement](https://www.capcom.co.jp/ir/english/news/html/e260316.html) reports Requiem's six-million-unit milestone; [FromSoftware's July 24, 2025 announcement](https://www.fromsoftware.jp/ww/pressrelease_detail.html?tgt=20250724_eldenring_nightreign_salesdata) reports Nightreign's five-million shipment milestone. These are dated historical milestones, not current demo-shop demand. Do not paste either number into the synthetic order dataset.

Only names and text metadata are used. No game artwork, logos or screenshots are redistributed. Game rights remain with their owners. The repository's NeoBase attribution and license remain in LICENSE/NOTICE/README.
